Hi, I'm Maya Cole!

Product Designer

Catharsis - Working to repair relationships

A mobile app that fosters genuine conversations by teaching communication skills. It provides users with practical exercises and real-time feedback to enhance their interactions.

Dartpop - Thrifting for students

A secondhand e-commerce app for Dartmouth students to list and buy items (ranging from clothes to textbooks) from each other, allowing them to update their wardrobes and get rid of clothes they no longer wear during the term.

Biblio - Community book exchange

A dynamic platform that allows users to trade, discover, and connect over books. It helps users save money, encourage more reading, reduce overconsumption, and build a community of book lovers.

Collis After Dark- late-night social and entertainment events

A collection of promotional posters designed to encourage the Dartmouth student population to engage in non-Greek life activities, fostering inclusivity, diverse social experiences, and a broader sense of community.

About Me

I’m a multidisciplinary designer with experience in product/graphic design, motion graphics, and 3D modeling. I love blending art and technology to tell stories and create meaningful impact. Excited to create and improve the lives of others through design!

Beyond design and development, I love experimenting with different creative mediums. One of my favorite hobbies is upcycling clothes.
